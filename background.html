Frequently asked questions

How do I change the background color of a product image?

Changing the background color of a product image is quick with Photoroom. Upload your image to Photoroom and choose a new background color from the palette to update your image instantly, without manual layers or cutouts.

Can I change the background color of a product photo without affecting the object?

Yes, you can change the background color of a product photo without affecting the featured object. Photoroom uses AI to detect the subject of your photo, so you only replace the background while the product, its edges, and details stay sharp and untouched.

Which background colors work best for product photos?

Neutral backgrounds like white , light gray, or soft beige work well for most product photos and are often preferred on marketplaces. You can also use brand colors or subtle tones, as long as there is enough contrast for the product to stand out clearly.

Is there a free online tool to change the background color of a picture?

Photoroom is a free online photo editing tool that lets you change the background color of your images. Upload your picture and then instantly replace the background with any color you need.

Can I change background color to a custom brand color?

Yes, you can set a custom brand color by choosing a specific shade from the color picker or entering a color value. You can reuse that color across your product photos to keep your catalog consistent.
